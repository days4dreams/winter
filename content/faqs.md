## Staying at Winter's Nook

### What time is check-in and check-out?
show on: home, enquire
Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is by 11:00.

### How do we get in?
show on: home
Winter's Nook has self check-in with a keybox by the front door, so there's no need to meet in person and the flat is all yours. We'll send you the code on the morning you arrive.

### Is there parking?
show on: home, flat, long
The flat is on a pretty, pedestrian-only street, so there are no cars outside. With a council guest permit, you can park on any of the nearby streets marked Zone G3, a minute's walk away. Ask us for a permit at least 5 days before you arrive, with your car's make, model and registration; there's a £80 holding deposit, returned when you leave the permit in the flat. Without a permit, there's parking at Gosforth Library (Hedley Terrace) and Salters Road Car Park.

### Are there stairs?
show on: flat, long
Yes. Winter's Nook is an upstairs Tyneside flat in a building around 100 years old, so the stairs are steep and doorways can be narrow. It may not suit anyone who finds stairs difficult.

### Is there Wi-Fi?
show on: flat, long
Yes, free Wi-Fi throughout. You'll find the details inside when you arrive.

### Can we smoke or vape?
Winter's Nook is non-smoking and non-vaping.

### Where can we buy food?
show on: flat, long
A well-stocked Spar on Salters Road is the closest, with a Post Office and cash machine. ASDA, M&S and Sainsbury's are all just off Gosforth High Street, and Ashburton Road has great local grocers and butchers. Our [guide to eating out](/the-guide/eating-and-drinking-in-gosforth) has our favourite cafés, pubs and restaurants.

## The Flat

### How many people can stay?
show on: flat, long
Up to two guests, in a king-size bed. We ask that no more than two people stay at any one time.

### What's in the kitchen?
show on: flat, long
An oven and hob, fridge, washing machine and kettle, with pots, pans, crockery, cutlery and glasses, and a dining table set for two.

### How is the flat heated?
show on: flat
All the heating is electric and warms up quickly. Each room has its own panel heater, and the bathroom has a wall heater.

### Is there somewhere to hang and store clothes?
show on: flat, long
Yes. The bedroom has a generous run of built-in wardrobes, so there's plenty of room to unpack properly. There's also a hairdryer, travel iron and board.

## Longer stays

### Do you offer discounts for longer stays?
show on: home, long, enquire
Yes. Stays of two weeks or more are 10% off, and monthly stays are 15% off. [Tell us your dates](/enquire) and we'll come back with our best rate.

### Who usually stays?
show on: long
NHS and healthcare staff, contractors, students and researchers, and people between homes: anyone who needs somewhere comfortable to come back to while they work or study away.

### How do I get into Newcastle?
show on: home, long
Regent Centre Metro is about ten minutes' walk, and from there it's around ten minutes to the city centre. Over 20 bus routes stop within ten minutes' walk, too.

## Booking

### How do payment and cancellation work?
show on: enquire
When you book direct, we take 30% when you book and the rest before you arrive. Cancel more than 14 days before your stay at no cost; after that, we keep the 30% paid at booking. For longer stays, we'll agree a payment schedule with you. Our [terms](/terms) have the full details.
