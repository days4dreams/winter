We're passionate about doing what we can for the planet and our local area. From sustainable toilet rolls to refillable, vegan, UK-made cleaning products, we try to tread lightly. Here are a few ways you can join in.

## Leave *the car*

Head out on foot or by Metro. Gosforth High Street is about fifteen minutes' walk, and the city centre about ten minutes on the Metro. Borrow a bag for life from the kitchen cupboard before you hit the shops.

## Shop *local*

We love Ashburton Road for food sourced in the region. Finished your book? Leave it on the shelf for the next guest and take another.

## Switch *off*

Swap the laptop for a stroll through Gosforth Central Park, or turn the TV off for an evening of games.

## Little *habits*

Separate your waste, making sure recycling is clean and dry, and set a timer when you switch on the heaters. They're easy to forget!
